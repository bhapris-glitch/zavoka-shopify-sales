Pricing questions

What is included in the trial?

The 5-day trial includes the full Premium feature set and 50 AI chats. There is no charge during the trial.

What is the refund policy?

All paid plans are non-refundable except where applicable law gives you a cancellation, withdrawal, or refund right. If the law that applies to your purchase provides a 14-day cooling-off or withdrawal right, you may request a refund within that legally applicable period, subject to any lawful conditions or exceptions. Any other mandatory legal rights also apply. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Can I cancel my plan at any time?

Yes. Turn off renewal in the Billing section of your merchant dashboard. Access normally continues through the paid period already in progress. Cancellation alone does not automatically refund a payment; see our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Mandatory legal rights still apply.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. In Billing, choose another plan and select “Switch plan now.” The change takes effect immediately. Stripe may invoice a prorated amount, including applicable taxes, and applies eligible unused-time credit. Your billing cycle restarts on the switch date, with the next renewal one month later. Review Stripe’s payment confirmation for the exact amount.

What happens when my trial ends?

Your assistant pauses after 5 days or 50 chats, whichever comes first, until you choose a paid plan.

Is yearly billing available?

Plans are currently billed monthly. Annual billing is not currently offered.
